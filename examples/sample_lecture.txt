Integrated Marketing Communications

Integrated Marketing Communications (IMC) is the coordination of all promotional
tools so that a company delivers a clear, consistent, and compelling message about
its products. The objective of IMC is to build strong customer relationships by
speaking with one voice across every channel.

The Promotion Mix

The promotion mix is the set of tools a company uses to communicate customer value
and build customer relationships. The promotion mix has five main components:
advertising, sales promotion, personal selling, public relations, and direct marketing.

Advertising is any paid form of non-personal presentation of ideas, goods, or services
by an identified sponsor. Television commercials and print ads are examples of advertising.

Sales promotion consists of short-term incentives to encourage the purchase of a product.
Coupons and discounts are examples of sales promotion.

Personal selling is a personal presentation by the firm's sales force for the purpose of
making sales and building customer relationships.

Public relations involves building good relations with the company's various publics by
obtaining favorable publicity and building a good corporate image.

Direct marketing means connecting directly with carefully targeted individual consumers to
obtain an immediate response. Email campaigns are an example of direct marketing.

Push and Pull Strategies

A push strategy uses the sales force and trade promotion to push the product through
channels. A pull strategy spends heavily on advertising and consumer promotion to build
consumer demand, so that consumers pull the product through the channel.
